ReminderFlow

Privacy Policy

Developer: Synvora Labs · Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ReminderFlow handles information when you use the application.

1. Introduction

ReminderFlow, developed by Synvora Labs, is a task and reminder management application designed to help users organize reminders, categories, notifications, and related attachments.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

ReminderFlow stores application data locally on your device. This may include:

  • reminders created by you;
  • custom categories;
  • reminder dates and scheduling information;
  • application preferences and settings;
  • trial and Premium status information, including local information used to manage purchase verification;
  • attachments selected by you and references to those attachments;
  • backup and restore information created through the application.

Synvora Labs does not operate a cloud account service for storing your ReminderFlow reminders, categories, or attachments.

3. Attachments and Files

ReminderFlow allows you to select files and attachments from your device. Access to these files occurs when you explicitly select them using the device's file selection interface.

ReminderFlow does not require broad access to your photo, video, audio, or media library.

Selected attachments may be opened, saved, exported, or shared with other applications when you explicitly choose an available action. The availability of these actions depends on your installed app version.

4. Notifications

ReminderFlow uses local device notifications to deliver reminders that you configure.

Notification scheduling and reminder information are handled on your device. ReminderFlow may also use Android system functionality to restore scheduled notifications after the device is restarted. Delivery can depend on device permissions and system settings.

5. Backup and Restore

ReminderFlow may allow you to create backup files containing application data and to restore data from backup files selected by you.

Exported backup files remain under your control. ReminderFlow does not automatically upload these files to servers operated by Synvora Labs. Depending on your device settings and Android configuration, operating-system backup or device-transfer services may separately process eligible app data under their own terms.

Application-created backups are not intended to transfer or grant paid entitlements; Premium access is determined separately through the applicable purchase-verification process.

6. Premium Purchases and Google Play Billing

ReminderFlow offers an optional Premium Lifetime purchase through Google Play and may provide a time-limited trial of Premium features.

Purchases are processed by Google Play. Synvora Labs does not directly collect or process your payment card details.

Google Play may provide the application with purchase status information necessary to determine whether Premium features should be available to you. The application may retain limited local purchase-verification state to support entitlement checks.

Google's handling of payment and account information is governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

7. Advertising and Tracking

ReminderFlow does not contain third-party advertisements.

ReminderFlow does not use advertising identifiers for advertising, behavioral profiling, or advertising tracking.

ReminderFlow does not include third-party advertising or analytics services for tracking user activity.

8. Support Communications

If you choose to contact Synvora Labs for support, ReminderFlow may open your device's email application with a prepared support message.

Information is sent to Synvora Labs only when you choose to send the email. This may include information that you voluntarily provide, such as your message, Google Play order ID, or details about a problem.

Support communications are used to respond to your request and resolve the reported issue.

9. Data Sharing

Synvora Labs does not sell your personal information.

ReminderFlow does not automatically transmit your reminders, categories, attachments, or exported backup files to Synvora Labs.

Information may be processed by third-party services when required to provide functionality that you explicitly use, such as Google Play Billing, or when you choose to share a file through another application.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

ReminderFlow data stored locally remains on your device until you remove it, clear the application's data, restore or replace application data, or uninstall the application, subject to any operating-system backups you have enabled.

Deleting local app data or uninstalling ReminderFlow does not itself cancel or refund a Google Play purchase. Purchase records are managed through Google Play.

Information voluntarily sent to Synvora Labs by email may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the support request and maintain relevant support records.

11. Children's Privacy

ReminderFlow is a general productivity application and is not specifically designed or directed toward children.

The application does not use advertising, behavioral profiling, or advertising tracking.

12. Security

Synvora Labs takes reasonable measures to design ReminderFlow so that access to user information is limited to what is necessary for the application's functionality.

Users are also responsible for maintaining the security of their device and any backup files they create.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when ReminderFlow functionality, privacy practices, or legal requirements change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
